# objective
Simulate four foundational quantum mechanics experiments using Qiskit. You will need to "bridge the gap" between the abstract principles of quantum physics and their practical implementation in code.
## Double-Slit Interference
Simulate quantum superposition and interference by applying a series of Hadamard and phase (RZ) gates to a qubit. You must measure the final probability distribution.
## Schrödinger’s Cat
Simulate a quantum superposition entangled with a "poison" mechanism using Hadamard and CNOT gates. The goal is to observe the superposition collapse upon measurement.
## Bell Inequality
Create a Bell state and demonstrate quantum entanglement by applying Hadamard and CNOT gates and then measuring the correlated outcomes of two qubits.
## Aharonov-Bohm (AB) Effect
Simulate a phase shift caused by a vector potential, even without a magnetic field, by using Hadamard and phase (RZ) gates.
