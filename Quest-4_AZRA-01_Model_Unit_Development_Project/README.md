# Objective
To design, develop, and present a highly articulated, visually striking, and functionally modular mecha model unit (Code: AZRA-01) based on provided artistic concept sketches. 
This unit will serve as the flagship "High-Mobility Warrior Mecha" basic unit for future company assembly models.
## Side Quest 1: "Core Chassis Blueprint" - Basic Unit Design
Objective: To establish the foundational aesthetic and structural elements of the AZRA-01's core body, ensuring both mobility and visual appeal.
* Character Appearance Definition: Design the overall aesthetic with white short hair and a mecha-like, two-dimensional anime character style.
* Mecha Configuration Detailing: Detail the upper body mechanical armor, leg mechanical armor, and a designated weapon backpack.
* Anatomical Rendering: Ensure natural, sculpted "river-style" abdominal muscle lines are present and not obscured by armor.
## Side Quest 2: "Arsenal & Adornment" - Weaponry and Paint Scheme
Objective: To design and specify the primary and interchangeable weaponry, along with a distinct and thematic paint job that enhances the mecha's combat aesthetic.
* Primary Weapon Design: Detail a silver and black assault rifle.
* Interchangeable Weapon Design: Detail a large, twin-bladed tactical knife.
* Paint Detailing: Specify red flame-baked paint accents on the forearm and lower leg armor sections.
* Color Scheme Finalization: Define the overall color palette as metallic gray, black, and red, emphasizing mechanical texture and a combat-ready atmosphere.
## Side Quest 3: "Emotional Core & Modularity" - Expression & Interchangeable Parts
Objective: To design and define the complete set of interchangeable parts, focusing on emotional expressions and hand configurations, to maximize the model's poseability and character.
* Expression Parts Design: Create designs for three distinct facial expressions: serious, happy, and crying.
* Hand Replacement Parts Design: Design three sets of black five-fingered hands: relaxed, clenched fist, and weapon-holding.
## Side Quest 4: "Gallery Showcase" - Posing and Official Photography
Objective: To professionally pose the completed AZRA-01 model and capture two official, dynamic promotional photographs.
* Posing Style Definition: Establish dynamic combat poses for the model.
* Photography Setup: Prepare a studio lighting environment with a gray background.
* Official Photo Capture (x2): Act as the model photographer and capture two compelling official display images
