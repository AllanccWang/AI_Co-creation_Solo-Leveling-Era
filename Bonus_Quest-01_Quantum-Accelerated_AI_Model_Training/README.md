# Goal
This bonus quest will test your ability to bridge the gap between two different fields: AI and Quantum Computing.
Your objective is to explore the potential of using a quantum circuit to enhance the performance of a classical machine learning model.
# Quests
## Main Quest: Quantum-Accelerated AI Model Training
- **Objective**: Use your **Quantum Computing Application** and **Quantum Machine Learning** skills to develop a hybrid quantum-classical model.
- **The Problem**: A basic machine learning model is struggling with a complex classification task. You believe a quantum layer can help.
- **Mission**: Design a simple quantum circuit (using Qiskit) that acts as a "quantum feature map." This circuit will process the input data and then feed its output to a classical neural network for final classification. The goal is to use the unique properties of quantum states to improve the model's accuracy.
- Notes: Unable to complete this quest on my own, I turned to generative AI for assistance. Below are straightforward approaches to bring the project to life.
  
Advice: A Simple Quantum-Classical PoC
- The key is to start small. Don't aim to solve a massive problem; aim to prove that you can successfully **bridge the quantum and classical worlds**.
## Side Quests
- **Side Quest: Code Integration**

Objective: Utilize your **API Integration** and **Program Development** skills to seamlessly connect the Qiskit quantum circuit with a classical machine learning framework like Scikit-learn or PyTorch.
- **Side Quest: Performance Analysis**

Objective: Apply your **Data Analysis** skill to evaluate the performance of your new hybrid model. You'll need to compare its accuracy and training time against the original, purely classical model. The results will prove if your quantum approach offers a tangible benefit.
- **Side Quest: Visualizing a Hybrid Model**

Objective: Use your **Digital Artistry** skills to create a compelling visual representation of your hybrid quantum-classical architecture. This visualization should clearly explain how data flows from the classical input, through the quantum circuit, and into the final classical layer.
